# DevBoard R&D and quality workbench

English | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

An R&D and quality workbench demo for a rail door-system manufacturer: review gates that follow the project kind, multi-discipline traceability, type tests and first-article inspection, and the loop from field alarms back to design. **The company "Ningchuan Transportation Equipment Co., Ltd." (宁川交通装备股份有限公司), its customers, metro lines, trains, parts and people are fictional, and all data is simulated**; no external system (PLM, ERP, QIS, SLM, MES …) is contacted. Gate names, limits and safety-integrity statements are assumptions ("inferred" or "project specification assumption"), not any real company's process.

After installing and adopting DevBoard 3.7.2 on the Agents page, the workbench shows ten pages and an **R&D Coordinator** agent. Four projects are seeded:

The board polls every 10 seconds. The demo-role switch signs decisions and sets the role lens below; it is not a login or a security boundary.

## Role lens

Switching the demo role changes what the pages show. A lens is only a view: it never changes who may act or what the server accepts.

- **Portfolio** opens with "{role}的工作台": a "waiting for me" count (approval nodes, gates and human-intervention requests), two or three counts of open records that matter to the role (for 质量工程师: open defects, 8D problems and trial reports to review) and up to five urgent related records, late ones first, each linking to its detail. The project cards follow under "项目组合".
- **Traceability, Tasks, Changes, Defects & tests, Trials & first article and Service & quality** have a toggle "只看与我相关（N）｜全部（M）", on "only mine" by default and back to it when the role changes. When nothing on the page concerns the role, the page says so and offers "看全部" instead of an empty table. The development-requirement view of Traceability is not filtered.
- **Projects** highlights the role's stages (as author) and gates (as approver or stage author) without filtering. **R&D panorama** starts its blocker-role filter at the demo role, or at no filter when that role has no blockers. **My to-dos** is unchanged.

The rules live in `client/lens.js`: 质量工程师 sees every defect, 机械设计师 only the mechanical requirements, 配置管理员 the configuration baselines, 客服工程师 only the alarms reported by customer service; 技术负责人, 分技术中心主任 and IPMT also see everything waiting for their decision. A record kind or role without a rule counts as not related.

## Demo guide

Next to the demo role at the top of every page there is a **演示指引** (demo guide) button. It opens a side panel to the right of the page. It is not a dialog, so the audience sees the page and the explanation together.

- **Main line**: 12 steps showing how one requirement becomes a running project. The project manager starts the project, IPMT approves CDCP, the systems designer works on requirements and traceability, the technical lead approves TR1, the software designer does design and development, the technical lead approves TR3, TR4 passes by itself once every code review finding is closed, the verification engineer records the acceptance evidence, the quality engineer approves TR5 and IPMT approves ADCP. Each step names the acting role and has a button to switch to it.
- **My role**: 4 to 6 getting-started steps for the current demo role, using records that role really has in the seed data. Switching the role changes the steps; for roles in the main line the last step points to the steps they own.
- Every step says what to do, what you will see and what it is worth in real work. "带我去" (take me there) opens the page and lights the target with an outline and a one-line bubble that fades after a few seconds; Esc or a click anywhere dismisses it. When the target is missing (for example no record on the page concerns the current role) the bubble appears next to the page title.
- Main-line step 2 opens the R&D Coordinator with the quick action prompt "新建并启动一个研发项目" filled in. It is never sent automatically. A full run with a real model takes tens of minutes, so for a short demo use **快进演示到…** (fast-forward the demo to…) on Portfolio, or stop at CDCP or TR1 and use the preset PRJ-2025-019 for the rest.
- Main-line step 7 points at the Tasks page of the live project and step 12 at the 交付成果 (delivered results) card on its project page.
- Main-line milestones tick themselves from the board: the live project exists, and CDCP, TR1, TR3, TR4, TR5 and ADCP are submitted and approved. The other steps are ticked by hand.
- The first time a demo role visits, the panel opens on the role tab at step 1. Ticks, the roles that have seen the guide and the "don't open by itself" switch are kept in this browser only (localStorage) and never written to the board; "从头开始" (start over) clears the ticks of the main line and of the current role.
- The only company named is the fictional Ningchuan Equipment; the "in real work" lines describe general value for a door-system manufacturer and the presenter relates them to a real customer.

## Rules

Every write goes through one set of functions in `domain.mjs`, shared by pages and agent tools.

Document release excludes the author's canonical role from proofreading, including labels such as `软件设计师（架构）` or a role followed by a person's name. An explicitly unknown author role must be corrected before starting the flow. Change-request rationale supplied in `note` remains in the history even when no state transition is requested.

- **Gate profiles** (`profiles.mjs`) are looked up by project kind: order projects (new build, retrofit, maintenance) run contract review, design liaison 1 and 2, design freeze, prototype review, type-test review, first-article approval and delivery acceptance; software projects run the IPD points CDCP, TR1–TR6, PDCP and ADCP. A stage can hold several gates, taken in order: approving a gate that is not the last returns the stage to in-progress with a history note ("TR2 已通过，进入 TR3"); the last one passes the stage and records a baseline.
- **Tailoring**: `project.tailoring.skipped` removes gates. A software project created on the board skips TR2, TR6 and PDCP by default; the seeded IPD project is untailored. Gate ids are `GATE-<project>-<code>-<n>`.
- **Approvers**: the decider's role must be one the gate names, otherwise `评审门不满足：本门需由 X 审批`; the authoring role of the stage can never approve. A co-signed gate takes one signature per role, is approved only when every role signed and every finding is closed, and any role rejecting rejects it (a comment is required). TR4 is marked `auto` and passes once its code-review findings are closed. A system-check finding is closed by an approver at the decision (or by its defect closing), never by the submitter.
- **Trials**: PLANNED → IN_PROGRESS → REPORTED → APPROVED (REPORTED → IN_PROGRESS for a retest). REPORTED needs a report number and a result; a failed trial is approved only when its change is approved. Submitting the type-test or FAI gate adds a finding for every trial of that kind not yet approved.
- **Field loop**: faults NEW → DISPATCHED → HANDLED → CLOSED; quality problems OPEN → ANALYZING → ACTION → VERIFYING → CLOSED (ACTION needs a root cause and D4; closing needs D8 and a closed change or a stated reason). A change from the field or a type test needs a root cause before analysis; approval issues the ECN and a new as-designed baseline and, for a project in service, does not send stages back to rework; verification is required before closing; retrofit progress only increases and completing it creates an as-maintained baseline.
- **Approval flows** (`approvals.mjs`, `approval-templates.mjs`): five templates (document release, ECR, ECN release, quality-problem closing, trial report) whose nodes are SERIAL, ALL (every role agrees once) or ANY. Only a role the current node names can act, otherwise `审批不满足：当前节点需由 X 处理`. Actions: agree; reject (comment required, the object returns to an editable state); send back (to the previous node, a named earlier node or the initiator; earlier agreements are voided but kept as trace); transfer (to another person of the same role); add a signer (a SERIAL node after the current one); comment; withdraw (initiator only, before anyone handled the first node). A node past its time limit is overdue, derived from the clock and never stored; the portfolio and the to-do page mark it red. One running flow per object.
  - Object states move only through their flow: CCB_REVIEW → APPROVED of a change only by an approved ECR (a direct move says `需走 ECR 审批`) and an ECR rejection rejects the change; the as-designed baseline is created when the ECN release flow is approved; a quality problem is CLOSED only by its closing flow and a trial REPORTED → APPROVED only by its report flow; an approved trial, a closed quality problem and either one under a running flow refuse edits; a deliverable is baselined when its release flow is approved and takes no new content while that flow runs, a gate submitted while the stage still has a deliverable with an unapproved release flow gets a finding, and that deliverable is not baselined by the stage passing. Gate co-signing is unchanged.
  - The agent can only start flows with `devboard_approval_submit`, and only for objects it drafted (under any acting role) or itself moved into the state the flow starts from, such as a customer change the presenter handed over; it has no handling actions. People handle them on My to-dos.
- **Traceability**: SWR-level items may be `SWR-`, `MER-` or `EER-` (software, mechanical, electrical) with a discipline; CUS→SYS, SYS→SWR and SWR→TC coverage; a requirements gate with CUS→SWR below 100% gets a finding automatically.
- **Changes**: SUBMITTED → ANALYZED (needs an impact analysis) → CCB_REVIEW → APPROVED / REJECTED → IMPLEMENTED → CLOSED. **Defects**: NEW → CONFIRMED → FIXING → RETEST → CLOSED, or DEFERRED.
- The v1 requirement, task and human-intervention machines are unchanged. Store schema is 4; a schema 3 store loads unchanged (no approvals yet), a store of another schema reads as the seed and is replaced on the first write (reads never write).

## Agent and live project

`coordinator` carries all 21 `devboard_*` tools, file and shell tools, `tool_search` / `tool_describe`, `subagent_spawn` / `subagent_list` / `subagent_collect` and the `devboard-playbook` skill (lifecycle, field loop, gate codes and co-signing rules). Its `devboard` preset pre-approves edits and writes inside the workspace; every shell command (including `node --test` and `node server.js`) asks for approval, because it runs code the agents wrote. Eleven declared roles run as fresh role children with the parent’s shared working directory and a role ∩ parent capability snapshot: Systems Designer (Requirements) `requirements`, Software Designer (Architecture) `architect`, Software Designer (Development) `developer`, Code Reviewer `reviewer`, Verification Engineer `tester`, and Mechanical Designer `mechanical`, Electrical Designer `electrical`, Diagnostics Engineer `diagnostics`, RAMS Engineer `rams`, Quality Engineer `quality`, Configuration Manager `config`. The code reviewer, quality engineer and configuration manager are read-only; the others can edit files. Mechanical, electrical, diagnostics and RAMS roles write under `devboard-projects/<project id>/analysis/<object id>/`, and the coordinator registers those files as `ANALYSIS` artifacts; the read-only quality and configuration roles return numbered findings in their answers without creating files. The field loop dispatches diagnostics, then RAMS, then quality review; a failed type test dispatches the mechanical or electrical designer for an impact note, then the configuration manager for a baseline check. All eleven are listed in `delegableAgents`; use a platform build whose package inspection accepts this complete list before adopting the application. Specialists have no shell or board tools and cannot approve or sign. They return commands to the coordinator for human approval and execution. All roles narrate in Chinese unless the user chooses another language, and use business labels instead of raw status enums.

**Fast-forward.** Next to 重置演示数据 on Portfolio, **快进演示到…** offers CDCP, TR1, TR3 and TR5 (each waiting for approval), 已完成 (ADCP approved), **TR3 待批 + 客户变更** (TR3 waiting, with a customer change already in CCB review and its ECR flow waiting in 我的待办) and **TR5 待批 + A 类缺陷** (TR5 waiting, with a class-A defect still being fixed). The two scenario values are `TR3_CHANGE` and `TR5_DEFECT`; the input is still only this enum. Choosing one opens the coordinator with the `fast-forward` quick action filled in; you send it. The coordinator calls `devboard_fastforward` once, whose only input is that gate. The tool creates a new live project every time, writes the stage's files from the sample project shipped in `assets/fastforward/` (documents in Chinese, a dependency-free Node app that honours `AGH_LOOPBACK_FD`, unit tests and a browser acceptance record about the fictional 车门健康监测看板) below the new `devboard-projects/<project id>/`, and moves the board through the real domain functions with back-dated timestamps, so gate rules, approver roles and traceability all apply. The target gate is left PENDING: approve it in 我的待办 and press "Continue" to carry on from there with the files already in place. Fast-forward never overwrites an existing directory; if a number was used before, it takes the next free one. 重置演示数据 empties the board but not the disk: delete `devboard-projects/` yourself when you want the files gone. This is the only reason the package declares `filesystem.write` on `devboard-projects/**` (it already declared read); the tool writes only the shipped templates and accepts no path or content.

**Tasks and defects.** After TR1 the playbook has the coordinator create one requirement per SWR group with a DEVELOPMENT and a TESTING task, move them to IN_PROGRESS and COMPLETED as roles work, and register each failed test case as a defect that walks FIXING, RETEST and CLOSED. Code review findings stay on the TR4 gate. Fast-forwarded projects carry the tasks from TR3 on and two defects from TR5 on (one closed, one B-severity waiting for regression).

**The live loop (3.7).** The board, the composer, the model and the board are wired together in both directions. (1) A work button hands an object to the coordinator, whose first call is `devboard_handoff({type, id})`: the decision, the open findings, the next stage with its deliverables, the open tasks, defects and changes, bounded in size. The platform's work object carries only a type and an id (`openApplicationWork` strips everything else), so the page reads the same summary from `devboard.handoff` and shows it next to the button before you send; nothing is ever sent for you. (2) Every action has an entry: draft an 8D from a fault or problem, draft a change from a failed trial (the old direct draft is now 手工起草), analyze a submitted or analyzed change, hand an answered intervention back, report a project's progress. (3) Every tool write leaves one server-built line (at most 160 characters, the newest 200 kept) in the **智能体动态** panel on Portfolio, with the optional `actingRole` (one of the eleven playbook roles) shown as the author; lines newer than the last visit are highlighted. (4) `devboard_test_record` takes `results [{tc, status}]`: counts are computed, disagreeing self-reported counts are refused, a passing TC becomes VERIFIED and an SWR when all its TCs are, and failed cases are listed for a defect the coordinator creates itself. (5) `devboard_artifact_record` also registers `CODE`, `TEST` and `ANALYSIS` files (inside the project directory, analysis only below `analysis/<object id>/`), previewed as plain text and never run or rendered. (6) When a person decides a gate, finishes an approval flow or answers an intervention, a **待交回研发协调员** card appears on Portfolio and My to-dos; one click opens the composer with that work attached. The card goes away when the coordinator next writes for that project, or when dismissed. Running the whole flow with a real model (CDCP to ADCP) is still a manual test.

**Customer change and rework.** From design on, a live project's page offers **客户提出变更**. It asks for a title, the customer's reason and the agreement clause, registers that change on the board (submitted, source customer) and opens the coordinator with the `customer-change` quick action filled in; you send it. The coordinator reads that change with `devboard_handoff` (it does not register another one), lets `traceFrom` trace its impact down the requirement chain (CUS → SYS → SWR → DES and TC, plus the deliverables and code modules they touch; `devboard_change_upsert` gains this optional flag, no new tool), moves it to CCB review and starts its ECR flow, then stops. Approve the flow in 我的待办 (co-signs, then the CCB node): the requirement items become CHANGED, the impacted stages that have passed **or are in review** go back to rework and their gates are superseded (a gate still pending is withdrawn), and "Continue" has the coordinator change only the affected items and resubmit the gates citing the change id. The change detail page draws the impact tree.

**Class-A defects hold the gates.** TR5 and ADCP cannot be approved while the project has an unclosed class-A defect (a deferred one is still unclosed). Submitting such a gate adds a system finding per defect (class-B defects get an advisory finding that people may close; TR4 is not affected). The rule is checked against the defects at the last signature, so closing the system finding by hand changes nothing: the refusal names the defect and says to fix it and pass the regression. Closing the defect on 缺陷与测试 closes the findings that name it, as 系统（缺陷已关闭）.

**Delivered results.** Once TR5 is submitted, or the project is done, its project page shows a **交付成果** card: the app entry file and how to start it, the latest test run of each level, the test report and browser acceptance record as previews, and the registered address, labelled as reachable only during the run that registered it. **启动并打开** opens the coordinator with the `run-app` quick action: it starts the app under shell approval as the playbook describes, registers the URL it prints and keeps it running until you say stop. **启动并打开** only drafts a request to the coordinator: you must send it and approve one shell command before an address appears, and that address works only during that run. Local services currently run only under the macOS sandbox, so rehearse on the demo Mac; the card's test runs, report and acceptance record are static and work anywhere. The page itself starts no process.

A software project created on the board (the default kind) follows the SOFTWARE profile with the default tailoring, is written under `devboard-projects/<project id>/` and is run by the coordinator; order kinds only create records. Role child agents only write files; only the coordinator writes to the board. After submitting each gate the coordinator ends its turn; a person decides on the board, then presses "Continue", which opens a coordinator draft carrying the gate id for the person to send.

In the field loop the coordinator only drafts: it reads alarm aggregates (`devboard_fault_list`) and trials, drafts D2–D4 and the change impact analysis citing alarm and trial ids, and never closes a problem, approves a trial or decides a change.

Explicitly named, authorized tools are offered directly, including the declared subagent and job tools; no search/describe round trip is required. `tool_describe` remains available for authorized deferred schemas, without granting access to tools outside the coordinator's allowlist.

The preset explicitly requests reviewed `sandbox.loopback_serve` under L1 enforcement with `on_unavailable: deny` and no external network allowlist. Command approval remains mandatory. The supported macOS Host binds the loopback socket and passes `AGH_LOOPBACK_FD` and `AGH_LOOPBACK_URL`; the generated Node server must use `server.listen({ fd: Number(process.env.AGH_LOOPBACK_FD) })` and print the supplied URL once ready. Keep the normal `127.0.0.1` / `--port` branch only for deployments without activation. Fresh binds and initiated connections remain denied; unsupported sandbox backends refuse this capability rather than falling back. Use a platform build supporting this capability before the live demo.

A real model may need tens of minutes for the whole flow.

Run commands from the correct directory: shell defaults to the workspace root. Pass actual test files, for example `node --test devboard-projects/<project id>/test/unit.test.js`, or set shell `cwd` to `devboard-projects/<project id>/` and run `node --test` for discovery. Do not pass the `test/` directory as a positional argument. Set project `cwd` for `node server.js --port <free port>` too; use the Host-provided listener and actual URL in AGH, or bind only `127.0.0.1` without activation. `--port` does not select the Host-allocated port. Never use ports 4177/4178. No external network or package installation is needed. Command-side `curl`/`fetch` cannot connect even to this listener; use the real browser for public HTTP acceptance and keep its results separate from `node --test` unit results.

If role delegation fails, mark role independence as unmet and keep the quality finding open; coordinator self-review cannot replace an independent role. Listener or browser failures remain acceptance blockers. Do not close a finding merely by moving it to a later stage, or register an unverified URL.

Long documents are written in sections: create a heading, outline and section placeholders with `write`, then append or replace one section at a time with `edit` (roughly 2–4 KiB per operation). Read each section back before reporting it complete. For this small project, keep SAD around 8–12 KiB; split a long section further instead of sending a whole document in one model output. A truncated output is an incomplete deliverable, never a successful write.

Before any `subagent_collect`, call `subagent_list` and copy the real child id exactly from that result. This also applies when `subagent_spawn(wait=true)` returns a running task; never invent or reconstruct an id. Verify both completion and the actual files.

### Presenter checklist

- Use an isolated home and workspace, a configured real model, the Host-allocated loopback URL (or a free fallback port for ordinary deployment) and one or two browser tabs. Review the installed package version and permissions before adopting it.
- Start a fresh browser context with locale **`zh-CN`** (for Playwright: `browser.newContext({ locale: 'zh-CN' })`), or set Simplified Chinese as the browser’s preferred language before opening the workbench. Also select **简体中文** in the platform settings language picker: a fresh platform preference defaults to English independently of the browser locale. Check the actual navigation, role task cards and platform labels in Chinese; record any labels that remain English. Chinese model narration does not set either preference.
- Rehearse all gates with typed role task cards and verify parent/child file visibility. Check Chinese progress narration and business state labels.
- Review each command and cwd before approval. Avoid unnecessary shell probes such as `node --version`; commands execute generated code. Tainted results can also require approval for later writes or board operations; read the action title and evidence.
- Before gates 4–5, require real HTTP test logs and browser evidence, resolve findings, verify the registered URL, and retain timing per gate. Confirm how to stop the background job.

## To confirm with the customer

Everything below is an assumption in this demo and should be confirmed before real use:

## Layout

- `profiles.mjs`: roles, organisations, gate profiles and the default live tailoring.
- `approval-templates.mjs`, `approvals.mjs`: the five approval flow templates (pure data) and the flow rules (agree, reject, send back, transfer, add signer, comment, withdraw, derived timeout); the link to object states is in `domain.mjs`.
- `blockers.mjs`: derives the blockers (never stored) that the snapshot and the R&D panorama page show.
- `domain.mjs`: stages, gates, traceability, changes, defects, trials, faults, problems, baselines, tests, the v1 machines, metrics and storage (chunked under the 64 KiB value cap).
- `seed-data.mjs`, `seed-docs/`: the fictional portfolio and its document bodies.
- `handoff.mjs`: the bounded hand-off of a gate, project, change, trial, fault, problem or intervention (`devboard_handoff`, `devboard.handoff`).
- `fastforward.mjs`, `assets/fastforward/`: the `devboard_fastforward` tool and the sample project it writes (documents, app, tests, acceptance record).
- `index.mjs`: profile services `devboard.snapshot`, `devboard.artifact`, `devboard.transition` (also trial, fault, problem and retrofit), `devboard.create`, `devboard.gate`, `devboard.approval` (start and handle approvals), `devboard.handoff` (read-only hand-off summary), `devboard.handback` (dismiss a hand-back card), `devboard.reset`, agent tools and the skill.
- `skills/devboard-playbook.md`: the coordinator skill.
- `client/`: the pages built with `@agnes/web-ui` (the delivery card is `result.js`, its pure helpers `delivery.js`, the fast-forward selector `fastforward.js`, the customer-change form `customer-change.js`, the change impact tree `impact-tree.js`; the activity feed `activity.js`, the hand-back cards `handback.js`, the hand-off buttons `work.js` with their table `entries.js`, the analysis files list `analysis.js`). The demo guide lives in `guide.js` (panel, spotlight), `guide-memory.js` (what the browser remembers), `guide-progress.js` (ticks from the board snapshot); the copy is plain data in `guide-data.js` (main line), `guide-optional.js` (the two optional scripts) and `guide-roles-*.js` (roles).
- `test/`: domain rule, plugin contract and seed data tests.
