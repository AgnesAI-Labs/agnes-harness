# Recruiting screen

Assess only explicit job-skill evidence. Exclude names, age, gender and school from scoring and prompts. Missing evidence is unknown. Show weights, quotes, unknowns and bias-safety notes. Never hire or reject automatically. Customer adaptation: validated job-related criteria, minimized data and consistent accessible human follow-up.
