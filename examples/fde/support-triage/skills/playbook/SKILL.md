# support-triage

Read the ticket; classify outage versus billing; route outages as urgent to incident response. Draft an acknowledgement with no invented fixes or refunds. Show the exact ticket and draft to the reviewer. Send only after Host approval. Check the receipt. Customer adaptation: replace the fixture lookup/send connectors, escalation taxonomy, SLA and customer-safe templates.
